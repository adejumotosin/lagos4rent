How trust works

Protection should be part of the product.

Lagos4Rent separates identity checks, property checks and reputation so renters can see exactly what has and has not been verified.

01

Know who is listing

Agents can undergo identity and profile verification. Paying for promotion never buys a verification badge.

02

See every major fee

Rent, agency, legal, caution, service charge and other fees are shown separately with a total move-in cost.

03

Review real interactions

Our review system is designed around completed platform connections rather than anonymous drive-by ratings.

04

Report suspicious behaviour

Users can flag scams, unauthorized listings, misleading fees, duplicates and harassment for review.

Before you pay anyone

Verification reduces risk. It does not replace due diligence.

Confirm the property, the landlord or authorized representative, the payment details and written terms. Tenant Direct means a tenant is introducing an opportunity, not that the tenant has authority to grant a tenancy.
